Child Safety Standards

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Zero tolerance for CSAE

Bridge prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, and content that sexualizes or facilitates the exploitation of children. This prohibition applies to real, illustrated, synthetic and AI-generated content.

2. Adults only

Bridge is intended for adults aged 18 and over. New users must pass the Bridge age gate, and the server rejects registration and user-generated-content actions for accounts that do not meet the 18+ requirement. The age gate is an access control, not a guarantee that a user cannot misrepresent their age.

3. Report content or a user

  • Use the in-app Report action on a post or comment, or Report user from a user's post.
  • For child-safety concerns, choose Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) where applicable and include useful details. Do not download, forward or redistribute suspected CSAM.
  • Email the Bridge safety contact at atlasthoughthelp@gmail.com.

4. Moderation and enforcement

Child-safety reports are treated as high-priority reports. Supported image media is checked by automated moderation before publication. Video uploads are disabled and rejected server-side. Reports are reviewed by authorized human moderators, and content or accounts found to violate these standards may be removed, restricted or permanently banned.

5. CSAM escalation and legal compliance

When we obtain actual knowledge of confirmed CSAM, authorized staff will promptly restrict or remove the material and account as appropriate, preserve necessary information, and follow the applicable reporting process. Where required by law, we will report through NCMEC's CyberTipline or to the relevant regional authority and cooperate with lawful law-enforcement requests. We do not treat an automated classifier result as a legal determination of CSAM.

6. Automated moderation is not the final decision

Automated systems are safety signals and can make mistakes. A classifier result, such as a possible minor or sexual-content signal, is not by itself a legal determination of CSAM. Reports and high-risk safety signals can be escalated for human review and enforcement.

7. Blocking and user safety

Users can block other users to reduce unwanted interaction and visibility. Bridge also provides reporting tools for posts, comments and user accounts so safety concerns can reach moderation.

8. Child-safety contact

Contact Bridge about child-safety concerns at atlasthoughthelp@gmail.com. Potential child exploitation can also be reported directly to NCMEC's CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org.

9. Standards

These standards are part of Bridge's safety rules and are intended to align with Google Play's Child Safety Standards and applicable child-protection laws. They are reviewed when the app's features, moderation systems or legal obligations change.
